Career Assessment Categories

Choose the assessment that matches your current stage and discover the ideal career path tailored for you.

Career Scan for High School Students

This assessment is designed specifically for high school students who are at a critical stage of choosing their academic direction. It helps students understand their natural abilities and interests before making important decisions about their future studies.

The Career Scan helps students identify:

  • Natural talents and innate abilities
  • Personality traits that influence career fit
  • Aptitude areas — logical, verbal, numerical, spatial
  • Preferred learning styles
  • Career interests and motivations

Career Scan for Higher Secondary Students

Higher secondary students face a pivotal decision — choosing the right degree program or professional course. This assessment provides clarity by mapping their aptitude and personality to suitable career streams.

The assessment helps students decide:

  • Best-fit degree programs and fields of study
  • Professional courses aligned with abilities
  • Specialization areas that match personality
  • Career direction based on aptitude analysis
  • Long-term career potential in chosen fields

Career Scan for Graduates

Graduates entering the job market need clarity on which career sectors suit them best. This assessment evaluates professional strengths and provides actionable guidance for career success.

The assessment helps graduates determine:

  • Best career sectors based on profile
  • Professional strengths and core competencies
  • Skill development needs for industry readiness
  • Suitable job roles and positions
  • Growth trajectory and career advancement paths

Employee Diagnostic Test

Designed for organizations, the Employee Diagnostic Test helps companies understand their workforce better. It provides insights into employee capabilities, helping optimize talent management and training investments.

The diagnostic test helps companies:

  • Assess employee strengths and capabilities
  • Identify skill gaps across teams
  • Plan targeted training programs
  • Improve workforce productivity and engagement
  • Make data-driven talent management decisions
