Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the AI SKILL SCAN Career Assessment Test.

AI SKILL SCAN is an advanced AI-powered psychometric career assessment system that evaluates your aptitude, personality traits, skills, strengths, weaknesses, and career interests. It generates a detailed career assessment report with personalized recommendations to help you make informed career decisions.

The test is designed for anyone looking to make informed career decisions. This includes high school students, higher secondary students, college students, graduates, job seekers, working professionals, and organizations looking to assess employee capabilities.

The test typically takes about 45 to 60 minutes to complete. The exact duration depends on the assessment category you choose. You can take the test at your own pace from any device with an internet connection.

Your career report includes a comprehensive analysis covering: aptitude analysis across multiple dimensions, personality profile, skill strengths and areas of improvement, career recommendations tailored to your profile, and development suggestions including courses and certifications.

Yes, the test is 100% online. You can take it from any device — desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone — with an internet connection. No need to visit a testing center or download any software.

Our recommendations are based on scientifically validated psychometric frameworks combined with advanced AI analysis. The AI processes your responses across multiple dimensions to generate highly accurate, personalized career suggestions backed by data-driven insights.

We offer four assessment categories: Career Scan for High School Students, Career Scan for Higher Secondary Students, Career Scan for Graduates, and Employee Diagnostic Test for organizations. Each category is tailored to the specific needs and decision points of the target group.

Schools, colleges, career counselors, and HR departments can all benefit from our platform. Visit our For Schools page or contact us for more information.
