# AI SKILL SCAN Career Assessment Website
## Functional Specification Document (FSD)

---

# 1. Project Overview

**Website Name:**  
AI SKILL SCAN Career Assessment

**Domain:**  
aiskillscancareerassessment.com

**Purpose:**  
The website promotes the **AI SKILL SCAN Career Assessment Test**, an AI-powered psychometric assessment system designed to evaluate individuals' aptitude, personality traits, skills, strengths, weaknesses, and career interests.

The system helps users make **informed career decisions** by providing AI-generated career recommendations.

The website acts as:

- A marketing website
- An information portal
- An entry point to take the career assessment test

**Primary Goal:**  
Convert website visitors into users who take the career assessment test.

# 3. Core Product

**AI SKILL SCAN Career Assessment Test**

AI-based psychometric assessment that evaluates:

- Aptitude
- Personality traits
- Skills
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Career interests
- Potential development areas

The system produces a **detailed career assessment report**.

---

# 4. Types of Career Assessments

The platform provides **four assessment categories**.

## 1. Career Scan for High School Students

Helps students identify:

- Natural talents
- Personality traits
- Aptitude areas
- Learning styles
- Career interests

Helps students decide future academic direction.

---

## 2. Career Scan for Higher Secondary Students

Helps students decide:

- Degree programs
- Professional courses
- Specialization areas

Provides career direction based on aptitude and personality.

---

## 3. Career Scan for Graduates

Helps graduates determine:

- Best career sectors
- Professional strengths
- Skill development needs
- Suitable job roles

---

## 4. Employee Diagnostic Test

Designed for organizations.

Helps companies:

- Assess employee strengths
- Identify skill gaps
- Plan training programs
- Improve workforce productivity
